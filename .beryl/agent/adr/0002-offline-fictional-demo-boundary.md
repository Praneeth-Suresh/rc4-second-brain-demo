# ADR 0002: Keep the Demo Offline, Fictional, and Read-Only

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The repository is a stakeholder-review demonstration of a proposed RC4 evidence-review workflow. It must prove neither NUS AI Know suitability nor real-data governance, security, retrieval quality, or user adoption.

## Decision

Use only local fictional fixtures and deterministic ES-module logic. Do not add network requests, external services, credentials, telemetry, browser persistence, real data, autonomous actions, or claims of enforced governance. Answers must be drafts grounded in eligible cited fixtures; review-due, unsupported, sensitive, and withdrawn-source cases must remain explicit.

## Consequences

- The demo is reproducible without credentials or network access.
- Simulated permission, freshness, and removal controls remain explanatory rather than enforcement evidence.
- A controlled pilot requires separate written platform confirmation, accountable ownership, corpus approval, operating procedures, and evaluation/stop criteria.
